What is the Indian Robotics Olympiad?

The Indian Robotics Olympiad (IRO) is a national competition where students showcase their skills in robotics, programming, and problem-solving.

Organized by ROBO-G, IRO features the ROBO Adventures categories: Explorers (ages 5–7), Innovators (ages 7–10), and Techies (ages 10–15).
